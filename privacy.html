The AI helper in plain words

The helper is optional and off until you use it. When you paste text and press the button, names, email addresses, phone numbers and web links are removed from the text first, and the rest is sent to Google's Gemini model, which acts as our processor and returns suggested fields. Nothing is saved until you confirm every field yourself. UniCircle does not keep the pasted text beyond the fields you save. You can withdraw consent at any time by not using the helper, or by switching it off or deleting what it filled in.

Jev assistant

Jev is an optional assistant for signed-in members: you can talk to UniCircle (the Jev button or the V key) or type to it. When you speak, your voice is turned into text by your browser's own speech service (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari); UniCircle never receives the audio. Simple commands such as "go to the map" are handled in your browser. Text Jev cannot handle there is sent to Google's Gemini model, our processor, after email addresses, phone numbers and web links are removed; we do not log that text. Jev may keep short notes about your preferences (for example "looks for finance mentors") in UniCircle so it can help you later. Only you can see them: under "Jev's memory" on your profile you can delete one note or everything, and they are deleted with your account. Spoken answers are read out by your browser and can be muted.

Location: city and neighbourhood only

The alumni map and the student-exchange map use only the city (and, if you choose, the neighbourhood) you type in yourself. We never ask for a street address, never use GPS or live location, and people in the same area are merged into one dot. The map cannot zoom to street level.

If you switch on your own pin on the member map, it is placed on a grid of about 5 km by our server, so we never store or show your exact position. It is off by default, only signed-in members with a verified email can see it, and you can hide or remove it at any time. You can move it once a day.

Where it lives

The website is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM and the database by Hetzner Online, both in Germany (EU). We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising trackers. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which see your IP address when your browser requests them.

How long

  • Waitlist entries: until you join, unsubscribe, or 24 months after sign-up, whichever comes first.
  • Accounts: until you delete your account; posts and messages are deleted with it.
  • Member data: see the last column of the table above for each category.

Deleting your account

Open your profile page and press Delete my account. This removes your profile and everything you own: photos, settings, points ledger, map pin, requests, events, RSVPs, mentoring plans and documents. Messages you sent disappear with the account, including in conversations other members are still in.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, export (portability) or restrict your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time (for location and the AI helper, switching them off or deleting the data is enough). Much of this you can do yourself in the product. For the rest, email hello@unicircle.eu and we will act within one month. You can also complain to your national data-protection authority.
