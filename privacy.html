Location: city and neighbourhood only

The alumni map and the student-exchange map use only the city (and, if you choose, the neighbourhood) you type in yourself. We never ask for a street address, never use GPS or live location, and people in the same area are merged into one dot. The map cannot zoom to street level.

Where it lives

The website is hosted by ALL-INKL.COM and the database by Hetzner Online, both in Germany (EU). We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising trackers. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and map tiles from CARTO/OpenStreetMap, which see your IP address when your browser requests them.

How long

  • Waitlist entries: until you join, unsubscribe, or 24 months after sign-up, whichever comes first.
  • Accounts: until you delete your account; posts and messages are deleted with it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object to processing — email hello@unicircle.eu and we will act within one month. You can also complain to your national data-protection authority.
