Method: WebSearch + WebFetch only. `[F]` = page opened and read; `[S]` = figure seen only in a search-result summary, page not opened or failed (treat as `sourced`, not `verified`). Several pages returned 403/404 (Tilburg alumni pages, UvA ABS /alumni, AMSIB, UCR for-alumni, EUR team page, WUR Connect news); those items are `UNVERIFIED`.
Scoring: MA1-MA8 weights from `00-intake/matching-criteria.md` (max 30). `(i)` = awarded on inference (no direct evidence of the negative, e.g. no platform named on the page). MA1 is `(i)` everywhere because alumni-office team size is not public for any institution screened. No named individuals are recorded; contact routes are public pages or roles.
Exclusion rule (criteria file): mature paid platform with multi-year contract = "displace later", not ranked. Where a platform is live but the contract is not public, the contract status is `UNVERIFIED` and the institution is flagged, not ranked.
