# UniCircle: Target Group Briefing, Segment B
## Persona: Mid-to-senior alumni in finance, management, law and consultancy who are protective of their inbox
Segment state: supply side of mentoring and tutoring; willing in principle, constrained by time and by professional confidentiality rules.
Proof basis: P2 (mentor flag, contact rules, status points on the live platform), P1 (premium tier as an "engaged, high-profile" group) · Confidence: low-med overall (regulatory facts high, behaviour inference) · Built: 2026-11-24 · Rounds: Claude web r1; Gemini cross-examination pending

Source files: `10-harvest/claude-r1-B-alumni-students.md`, `20-distilled/fact-cards-B.md`, `data.json`. Labels: **E** evidence (study, survey, regulator, official page), **I** inference, **O** practitioner opinion. UNVERIFIED = could not be confirmed. Segment level only; no named individuals.

---
## Purpose
Show UniCircle how to make supply attractive: a bounded, cheap contribution with visible status, protected by contact rules. Promise no large career effect for the student: mentoring effect sizes are small (E: attitudes .16-.19, career .05-.11, 116 samples, N=45,705).

## 2. Psychology and Emotional Architecture
### Deep Fears (I unless stated)
| Fear | Translation for design |
|---|---|
| Open inbox, bulk or "pick my brain" asks | Contact rules, weekly caps, a fixed 20-30 minute slot (time-box is O, UNVERIFIED as measured preference) |
| Disclosing market-sensitive or client information (B1, B3) | "No client or deal information" banner; conflict-declaration field; "no legal advice" disclaimer |
| Being asked for a job or investment (B2) | State the ask and outcome up front; 15-20 minute box |
| Billable-time loss (B4) | Structured asks: short written brief, 30 minutes, specific deliverable |

### Core Drivers
| Driver | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Mentor satisfaction and commitment | E: mentors report higher job satisfaction and commitment than non-mentors (meta-analysis) |
| Mixed motives: self-, protege-, relationship-, organisation-focused | E: 20 interviews (med) |
| Hiring pipeline (juniors, interns) | I |
| Status visible inside the firm or network | I: give mentors a self-focused reward next to the helping motive |

### Core Paradox
I: willing to help but unreachable by default; cold C-level outreach replies about 7% (vendor benchmark, low-med). Status points exist to make approach earned, not free.

## 3. Value Proposition Mapping (proof-anchored)
| UniCircle capability | Proof | Audience trigger | Key message (no figures beyond the research) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact rules | P2 | B1 and B3 confidentiality duties (E: banker's oath, MAR art. 14, Advocatenwet art. 10a) | You decide who can reach you and when |
| Status points as access ticket | P2 | Wants visible recognition (I) | Quiet status for giving time |
| Mentor flag with capacity limit | P2 | Time is the biggest barrier (I) | A bounded commitment |
| Map and small city events | P2 | Peer network across firms and cities (I) | Meet peers in your hub city |
| Case-clinic or mock-case slots (B4) | P2 tutoring format | Consulting recruits heavily from graduates (I) | One structured 30-minute slot |
### Positioning rules
- Never say "make a big difference to a student's career": the effect sizes are small. Say "a bounded, cheap contribution".
- Never imply employer CSR credit: UNVERIFIED for Dutch banks and Big Four; law firms count pro bono, not clearly mentoring.

## 8. Trust Vulnerability
- B1: E: all Dutch bank staff take the banker's oath or affirmation; unlawful disclosure of inside information is prohibited (MAR art. 14). Neutraliser: banner, conflict field, contact rules.
- B3: E: five core values incl. independence, integrity, confidentiality (Advocatenwet art. 10a); no mandatory pro bono hours and no CPD credit for pro bono in the Netherlands (older survey); one large firm reported 8,500+ pro bono hours, 75 matters, 211 lawyers in 2023 (firm self-report). Neutraliser: topic whitelist, conflict declaration, disclaimer.
- B2 and B4: confidentiality is a lesser concern (B2) or client-independence rules apply to audit-linked advisory (B4, I).
