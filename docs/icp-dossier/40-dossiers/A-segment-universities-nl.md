## 1. Who They Are (Firmographic Context & DMU Baseline)
- **Scale (verified or sourced, per institution):** Nyenrode 30,000; Utrecht 233,000+ (2025); RSM 55,000+; VU 154,769 (whole university); Leiden about 109,000; Twente 66,000+; Tilburg 87,000+ (2022 report); Erasmus 175,000+; TIAS about 25,000. Radboud, UCU, UvA: base not found.
- **Team size:** not public for any institution screened (inference that it is small; MA1 is inference everywhere).
- **Structure seen on public pages:** a central alumni office plus faculty or school networks (Utrecht: 9 faculty networks; Radboud: faculty alumni offices plus Alumni Office Management; VU SBE: Career Services and Alumni Relations team; Maastricht: an expertise centre for alumni, fundraising and lifelong development).
- **Decision-making unit:** not researched. No role-holders were recorded; first contact routes are public alumni pages or office roles only. DMU roles (initiator, decision-maker, buyer, gatekeeper) are therefore a gap, not a finding.
- **Investment and risk style:** university funds exist at most institutions (Utrecht EUR 2.8m raised in 2025; Tilburg EUR 358,778 in 2022; Radboud, Leiden, Nyenrode, Twente, Amsterdam funds named). No budget or price information was found.

## 2. Psychology and Emotional Architecture
Not researched in round 1. The harvest does not contain interviews or statements of fears or drivers of alumni-office staff.
What the research does show (observable behaviour, not psychology):
- Volunteers carry the network: RSM chapter leaders are volunteers, tools are LinkedIn, WhatsApp, WeChat, Line (verified); Maastricht Alumni Circles run by volunteer committees (sourced).
- Output can lapse: VU SBE's latest linked magazine edition is July 2023 (inference: low output).
- Intake evidence from one anonymised Dutch business-school alumni office (P1, confidential, not named): a very large alumni base next to a few thousand students, flat budget, part-time database role, a wish for alumni to find each other by sector and by tutorial group. Treat as one case, not a segment fact.

## 8. Trust Vulnerability
The research gives no direct evidence on what an alumni office distrusts. Two structural facts to design around: (1) many offices have public mentor programmes already, so UniCircle must connect to them, not replace them (inference); (2) data protection: the map shows only city or neighbourhood and the directory is members-only (P6, verified in session).
