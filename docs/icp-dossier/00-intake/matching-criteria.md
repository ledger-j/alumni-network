# UniCircle: Matching Criteria (every criterion links to a proof point)

## Segment A: universities and faculties (buyer side), focus Netherlands
| ID | Observable signal | Weight | Proof it transfers |
|---|---|---|---|
| MA1 | Large alumni base relative to alumni-office team (rough rule: > 20k alumni, < 6 FTE) | 5 | P1 |
| MA2 | No integrated alumni platform: relies on newsletter, LinkedIn group, spreadsheets or a legacy portal | 5 | P1 |
| MA3 | Cohort-based or problem-based teaching (tutorial groups, cohorts, colleges) so "who did you study with" is a real hook | 4 | P1 (tutorial-group spillover) |
| MA4 | A business school / faculty with its own alumni activity (SBE-like), not only a central office | 3 | P1 |
| MA5 | Public alumni or lifelong-learning ambition (strategy plan, "alumni" in 2025-2030 plan, mentoring programme, chapter network) | 4 | P1, P2 |
| MA6 | International alumni share or diaspora cities (e.g. Munich, London, Berlin, Brussels) | 3 | P1, P2 (map) |
| MA7 | Membership / premium / fundraising interest (alumni fund, paid network) | 3 | P1 (premium tiers) |
| MA8 | Student-side programme that mentoring or tutoring could plug into (buddy, peer tutoring, career services) | 3 | P2 |

Exclusions: institutions that run a mature paid alumni platform with a multi-year contract (note the vendor; log as "displace later").

## Segment B: alumni (supply side)
Types: **B1 finance**, **B2 management**, **B3 law**, **B4 consultancy**. Signals: 5-20 years after graduation, senior or partner-track role, already mentoring or hiring, protective of their inbox (this is why status points and contact settings exist). Proof: P2 (mentor flag, contact rules), P1 (premium = "engaged, high-profile" group).

## Segment C: students
Types (working heuristic, not a psychometric claim): **red** action-oriented, **blue** thinker, **social** connector. Signals: seeks tutoring, internships, peer groups. Proof: P2 (tutoring), P1 (tutorial groups). Each type must be mapped to observable platform behaviour (what they click first, what they ask for), not to personality labels alone.

Rule: a segment or account with no linked proof is labelled `speculative` and kept out of File C.
