| A2 A3 F5 A14 farming and double award | **accepted, scope cut.** Points only from a mentoring/tutoring closed by the mentor, after the mentorship was `active` for ≥ 24 h, one award per mentorship (unique `(user, reason, ref)`, written in a transaction), same pair at most once per 7 days, no award if the mentee earned from that mentor in 30 days, daily cap 15 per user, a closed mentorship cannot reopen.
