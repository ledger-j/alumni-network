The problem with rented networks

Your alumni are online. Your relationship with them isn't yours.

Most institutions reach their graduates through a third-party feed: a group page here, a mailing list there, a spreadsheet that was accurate the year someone graduated. The platform decides who sees your posts, and the data about your own community sits with someone else.

The result is familiar to every alumni office: invitations that bounce, chapters that can't find their members, and events where the right people never hear about it.

  • Records go stale. Graduates move cities and change jobs; the alumni database rarely hears about it.
  • Reach is rented. An algorithm you don't control decides how many alumni see your announcements.
  • Data sits elsewhere. Insight into your own community depends on another company's terms and export options.
  • Events miss their audience. Without current city information, reunions and chapters can't reliably reconnect a cohort.
  • Students and alumni stay apart. Mentoring runs on personal contacts and goodwill, so it's hard to scale beyond a few enthusiasts.

How a pilot works

Start small, learn together, then decide.

We're pre-launch and piloting with interested institutions. A pilot is shaped around your goals — one faculty, one cohort or one city is enough to begin.

  1. Scope & data importWe agree goals, the audience and the data-processing terms, then import only the alumni data you choose to bring.
  2. Branded launchWe open UniCircle to one cohort or one city, with an invitation sent in your institution's name.
  3. Mentoring & events runAlumni and students opt in to mentoring; you run chapter meetups or a reunion with live "who's attending".
  4. Review & decideWe review what worked together, in aggregate, and you decide whether and how to continue.
Privacy & compliance

Built for European data rules from the start.

Your data protection officer should be able to say yes quickly. These are design choices, not settings we hope you'll find. Read our privacy notice.

  • EU servers. Platform data is hosted in the European Union.
  • Data minimisation. We ask only for what the network needs to work.
  • City-level location only. City and neighbourhood — never a street address or GPS position. How the map works.
  • Consent-based. Mentoring, the map and messages are opt-in for every member.
  • Export & deletion. Institutions can export their data; members can ask us to export or delete theirs at any time.
  • No ads, no trackers. We never sell data and run no advertising trackers.
Questions from alumni offices

Frequently asked

Does UniCircle integrate with our existing CRM?

UniCircle is designed to sit alongside your existing alumni database, not replace it. For a pilot we start with an import of the records you choose, and we'll discuss how updates should flow back to your CRM as part of scoping.

What does it cost, and what are the pilot terms?

We're pre-launch, so there's no public price list yet. We'll tailor a pilot to your scope — faculty, cohort or city — and agree the terms with you up front. Write to us to start that conversation.

Who is the data controller and who is the processor under the GDPR?

The precise roles depend on how the pilot is set up and will be set out in the data-processing agreement we conclude with your institution before any data is imported. We'll work through it with your data protection officer.

Can we use our own branding?

Yes. Pilots launch with invitations and a presence in your institution's name, so alumni recognise who is reaching out. The exact branding options are part of scoping.

Who owns the data?

Your institution keeps control of its alumni relationship and the data it brings, and can export it at any time. Members control their own profiles and can ask us to export or delete them at any time.

Can several faculties or services take part?

Yes. A pilot can start with a single faculty, careers service or alumni office and widen to others later. We'll agree with you how faculties share or separate their communities.
