Questions

UniCircle, answered.

Does it cost anything?

Not for students: messages, the map, everything. Alumni aren't paid either. It's people giving back to their own community.

What can I ask an alumnus for?

Whatever they offer. Each alumnus writes one line on their profile, like a short call, a CV review or a coffee in their city. You ask for exactly that.

Can anyone see where I live?

No. You give a city and, if you like, a neighbourhood: never a street, never your live location. Your dot sits on the neighbourhood centre, merged with everyone around you, and only signed-in members see the names. You appear on the map only if you choose to.

What happens to my data?

It stays on servers in the European Union and is never sold. No ads, no trackers. You can export or delete it any time. Details in our privacy policy.

My city isn't open yet. What now?

We open city by city, in small waves. Join the waitlist with the city you're in or moving to: that tells us where to open next.

Who's behind UniCircle?

A small team of students and alumni, as a Beyond Borders Marketing Consultancy project. We're early, deliberately so: no bought followers, no invented numbers. Questions or ideas? Write to hello@unicircle.eu.

I work at a university. Can we use it?

Yes. We're piloting with a few institutions, and one faculty, cohort or city is enough to start. Your alumni keep their own details current and mentor your students, and your data stays yours, hosted in the EU. Talk to us about a pilot.
