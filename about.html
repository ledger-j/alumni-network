About UniCircle

A network for minds that connect, mentor and give back.

UniCircle is a European alternative to LinkedIn — built for one community, owned by the university, and designed to keep the people you studied with in your life for good.

The name

University & United, drawn into one Circle.

A circle has no start and no end — and neither does your relationship with the people you studied with.

UniCircle is the companion for your whole life cycle: from your first day as a student to every chapter that follows as an alumnus. A place to build connection, keep learning, and — just as importantly — have fun with the people who were there.

Our story & why we built it

It started in an alumni office. It outgrew one faculty.

UniCircle began as a simple idea inside a European business school's alumni office: richer alumni data, and genuinely more value for alumni, through a platform built for them rather than rented from a feed.

As we explored it, the idea outgrew a single faculty. It called for a broader scale — an independent platform that interconnects universities across Europe, and strengthens the network in two directions: between alumni, and between alumni and the students coming up behind them.

Why a university-owned network

Rented feeds can't reconnect people. Owned networks can.

◎

The university owns its data

The institution controls the alumni relationship instead of renting access through a third party — which matters for its mandate, its compliance and its long-term independence.

↺

It solves the event problem

Alumni keep their own details current, so the map, the city chapters and "who's attending" reliably reconnect people who studied together — the gap a broadcast feed can't close.

⇄

Two directions, one circle

Alumni help each other, and they help the next generation. Mentoring and student exchange are built in, not bolted on.

What we promise

Built with care, not for engagement metrics.

Every choice we make starts from one question: does this help someone reconnect, learn or give back?

  • European and private by design. Data stays on EU servers. We never sell it, and there are no ad trackers.
  • Location without surveillance. City and neighbourhood only — never a street, never GPS. How the map works.
  • No fake activity. No bought followers, no invented numbers. Example content is always labelled as a sample.
  • Opt-in everything. Mentoring, the map and messages are things you switch on, not things done to you.
  • Free for students. Students never pay to reach a mentor.
Who's behind it

A small team, building in the open.

UniCircle is a Beyond Borders Marketing Consultancy project, built together with students and alumni who wanted a better way to stay in touch. We're early — deliberately so. We'd rather build the right thing with the community than ship a polished shell.
