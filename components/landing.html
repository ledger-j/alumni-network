About UniCircle

A network for minds that connect, mentor, and give back.

The name

University & United, drawn into one Circle.

A circle has no start and no end — and neither does your relationship with the people you studied with. UniCircle is the companion for your whole life cycle, from your first day as a student to every chapter that follows as an alumnus: a place to build connection, keep learning, and — just as importantly — have fun with the people who were there.

Our story & why we built it

A European alternative to LinkedIn — where the university owns the graph.

UniCircle began as a simple idea inside a European business school's alumni office: richer alumni data, and genuinely more value for alumni, through a platform built for them rather than rented from a feed. As we explored it, the idea outgrew a single faculty. It called for a broader scale — quite possibly an independent platform that interconnects universities across Europe.

In short, we set out to build a European alternative to LinkedIn: one that gives universities back the ownership of their alumni relationship, and strengthens the network in two directions — between alumni, and between alumni and the students coming up behind them through active mentoring.

1 · The university owns its data

The institution controls the alumni relationship instead of renting access through a third party — which matters for its mandate, its compliance, and its long-term independence.

2 · It finally solves the event problem

Because alumni register and keep their own details current, the map, the city chapters and the "who's attending" view can reliably reconnect people who studied together — the exact gap a rented feed can't close, because it doesn't own the graph.
