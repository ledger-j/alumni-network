Alumni mentoring students

One conversation can save a student a year of guessing.

For students
  1. Find an alumna or alumnus by programme, sector or city.
  2. Send a short, structured ask — your goal and the format you'd like.
  3. Continue the conversation in Messages.
For alumni
  1. Switch on “Offer mentorship” in your profile.
  2. Say what you can offer — a call, a CV review, a coffee.
  3. Students reach you directly; you decide what to accept.
