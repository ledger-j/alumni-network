Mentors & tutors

One conversation can save a student a year of guessing — and a good tutor, an exam.

For students
  1. Find a mentor (alumni) or a tutor (students and alumni) by programme, subject or city.
  2. Send a short, structured ask — your goal and the format you'd like.
  3. Continue the conversation in Messages.
For alumni & tutors
  1. Switch on mentoring or tutoring in your profile.
  2. Say what you offer — a call, a CV review, a subject you tutor.
  3. Students reach you directly; you decide what to accept.
