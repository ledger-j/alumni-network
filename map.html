How a dot is made

Privacy isn't a setting here. It's how the map is built.

  1. You type a cityAnd, if you like, a neighbourhood. Never a street, never a postcode. No GPS, no live location — ever.
  2. We place a neighbourhood dotYour dot sits on the neighbourhood (or city) centre, not on you. Without a neighbourhood, you're simply part of the city.
  3. Neighbours mergeEveryone in the same area becomes one dot with a count. The more people, the less any single person stands out.
  4. Zoom stops at districtThe map can't zoom to street level. There's nothing finer to see, because nothing finer is stored.

You're in control

Your dot, your rules.

The map only works if people trust it. So every choice is yours, and every default is the private one.

  • Opt-in. You appear on the map only once you add a city to your profile.
  • Leave any time. Remove your city from your profile and your dot disappears.
  • Members only. Names behind a dot are visible to signed-in members of your circle — never to the public or search engines.
  • Coarse by design. City and optional neighbourhood; no street, no coordinates, no movement history.
  • EU-hosted. Data stays in Europe and is never sold. Privacy policy.
Questions

About the map

Can anyone see where I live?

No. We never ask for or store a street address. The finest level is a neighbourhood you choose to type in, shown as a shared dot with everyone else in that area, and the map can't zoom past district level.

Who can see that I'm in a city?

Signed-in members of your circle. Visitors to the public site only see illustrative sample dots like the ones above.

What if I don't want to be on the map?

Then you aren't. Leave your city empty (or remove it later) — you can still use mentoring, events and everything else.

My city isn't on the map.

We add cities as members join. Add yours to your profile or the waitlist and it will appear once there's a place to put it.

Does the map track me when I travel?

No. It doesn't use your device location at all. It shows what you typed, until you change it.
