01 · Mentoring

Students meet the alumni who were in their seat.

Alumni switch on “open to mentor” with a one-line offer — a CV review, a 20-minute call, a city tip. Students search by programme, sector and city, and ask in one tap. Opt-in on both sides, no cold messages, no paywall for students.

  • Matching by path — same programme, same sector, or the city you're moving to.
  • Small asks — every offer is scoped, so saying yes is easy.
  • Give back — mentors show up first on the map and in the directory.
